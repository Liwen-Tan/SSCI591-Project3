Exploring Parks in Los Angeles County

SSCI 591 | Web GIS Project 3

About This Map

This interactive map shows parks and open spaces across Los Angeles County. The park boundaries and related information are loaded from an online ArcGIS Feature Service. I modified the original Intro to FeatureLayer sample by changing the basemap, park symbols, and map extent. Users can click on a park to view its name, city, area, type, and agency. I also added Home and Scale Bar components to make the map easier to explore.
